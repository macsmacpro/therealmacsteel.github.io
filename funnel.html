Agent Rules

How the system stays clean

  • Every asset maps to one buyer, one offer, one CTA, one tracking id, and one owner.
  • Every offer must pass QA and E2E before promotion.
  • Every public claim must be proof-safe and comparison-guarded.
  • Every external send, post, deploy, trade, account mutation, or client-owned action must use the external-action guard.
  • Every scale decision must come from conversion, reply, revenue, retention, or verified visibility evidence.
  • If a needed tool is not free, procurement looks for a free option; if none exists, builder/production creates a free internal substitute.

Measurement

Scale from evidence

Traffic, clicks, intakes, purchases, replies, fulfillment, retention, upgrades, and referrals are tracked before the system scales an offer or campaign.

No keyword-only scaling. No fake demand. No paid-tool dependency.
