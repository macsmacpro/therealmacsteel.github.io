FULL BIO (300 WORDS)

Founded in 2026 by Mac Steel in Acworth, Georgia, SteelWorks Intelligence builds autonomous business systems for entrepreneurs and enterprises. Powered by open source AI infrastructure, their systems run 22 specialized autonomous agents across content, leads, real estate intelligence, and competitive monitoring — 24/7, at zero operational cost.

After years of paying $847/month for fragmented SaaS tools that didn't communicate with each other, Mac set out to build a single integrated autonomous system. SteelWorks Intelligence now runs 22 AI agents, 162 skills, and 54 scheduled operations 24/7 on self-hosted open source infrastructure at zero marginal cost.

The breakthrough that made the $0/month cost structure possible was a proprietary bridge architecture that routes AI queries to free AI browser sessions without touching paid APIs. This structural cost advantage cannot be replicated by cloud-dependent competitors without eliminating their own revenue model. Technical architecture details available under NDA for qualified media.

SteelWorks Intelligence operates on a 5-Level Recursive Self-Improvement roadmap: from autonomous operation (Level 1 — complete) to agents writing their own skills (Level 2 — building), through autonomous income generation (L3), recursive self-funding (L4), and ultimately autonomous capital allocation via Alpaca stock market investing (L5). Mac documents the entire build publicly at @therealmacsteel across YouTube, TikTok, X, and LinkedIn.

Mac is available for media interviews, podcast appearances, and conference speaking on the topics of AI automation, autonomous business architecture, and the future of work in the agentic AI era. He is based in Acworth, GA (Atlanta metro) and works with entrepreneurs and enterprises nationwide.
