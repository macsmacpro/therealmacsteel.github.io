FULL BIO (300 WORDS)

Mac Steel is a serial entrepreneur and AI systems architect based in Acworth, Georgia. He is the creator of OpenClaw — the first self-hosted Autonomous Business Operating System (ABOS) — a production-grade multi-agent AI platform that runs an entire business without daily human input.

After years of paying $847/month for fragmented SaaS tools that didn't communicate with each other, Mac set out to build a single integrated system to replace them all. The result was OpenClaw: 22 specialized AI agents, 162 skills, and 54 scheduled operations running 24/7 on self-hosted infrastructure at zero marginal cost.

The breakthrough that made the $0/month cost structure possible was a novel Chrome DevTools Protocol bridge architecture, which routes AI queries to GPT-4, Gemini, DeepSeek, Grok, and Perplexity through their browser UIs without touching paid APIs. This structural cost advantage cannot be replicated by cloud-dependent competitors without eliminating their own revenue model.

OpenClaw operates on a 5-Level Recursive Self-Improvement roadmap: from autonomous operation (Level 1 — complete) to agents writing their own skills (Level 2 — building), through autonomous income generation (L3), recursive self-funding (L4), and ultimately autonomous capital allocation via Alpaca stock market investing (L5). Mac documents the entire build publicly at @therealmacsteel across YouTube, TikTok, X, and LinkedIn.

Mac is available for media interviews, podcast appearances, and conference speaking on the topics of AI automation, autonomous business architecture, and the future of work in the agentic AI era. He is based in Acworth, GA (Atlanta metro) and works with entrepreneurs and enterprises nationwide.
