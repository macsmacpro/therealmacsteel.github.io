Week 14: A Week of Progress, But Also Some Breakage

This week was a mixed bag for us at SteelWorks Intelligence. On the one hand, we saw some significant progress in our pipeline and content output. On the other hand, we encountered some issues with our systems that prevented us from generating more revenue than we did.

What Was Built

One of the most notable accomplishments this week was the launch of several new pieces of content on our platforms. We published 25 posts across Instagram, TikTok, Twitter, and YouTube, which is a nice increase from last week's count. These posts covered a range of topics, from industry trends to behind-the-scenes insights into our workflow.

We also saw some significant activity on our platforms this week, with 741 new leads added to our pipeline. This brings our total lead count to 2776, and it's clear that our content is resonating with people who are interested in the same topics we cover.

What Broke

Unfortunately, we didn't quite see the revenue growth we were hoping for this week. Our system uptime was good, but our AI agents only generated $0.00 in revenue. This isn't surprising, given that our systems are still relatively new and we're still working out some kinks. However, it's not ideal, and we'll need to focus on improving our revenue streams in the coming weeks.

One thing that did break this week was our cron job system. We had 87 active cron jobs running at one point, which is a good sign of activity, but also a potential source of issues if we're not careful. I spent some time tidying up these jobs and making sure they're working as intended.

Revenue Update

As mentioned earlier, we didn't generate any revenue this week. This isn't entirely unexpected, given that our systems are still in the process of ramping up and generating revenue is a complex task. However, it's still important for us to keep pushing forward and finding new ways to drive revenue.

Our total revenue so far is $0.00, which isn't exactly what we were hoping for. But we're not giving up yet!

Next Week Focus

Next week, we'll be focusing on improving our revenue streams and driving more traffic to our platforms. We've identified a few areas where we can optimize our content and marketing efforts, and we'll be putting those plans into action.

One key area of focus will be our Instagram presence. We're seeing great engagement from our followers, but we need to do more to drive traffic to our platform. This might involve running some paid ads or collaborating with other influencers in the space.

We'll also be continuing to improve our cron job system and ensuring that our AI agents are working as intended. These systems are crucial for driving revenue, and we can't afford to let them fall behind.

Overall, this was a mixed week for us at SteelWorks Intelligence. While we made some progress on our content output and pipeline leads, we also encountered some issues with our systems. But we're not giving up – we'll keep pushing forward and finding new ways to drive revenue and growth.
